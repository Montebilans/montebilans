Accounting

Complete accounting support for your company.

Accounting services offered by Montebilans:

  • Bookkeeping services

    Keeping the general ledger and subsidiary ledgers (cash book, supplier and customer ledgers), VAT calculation, preparing financial statements and balance sheets.

  • Payroll and fees

    Calculating salaries, taxes and contributions for employees, as well as fees under service contracts, copyright agreements and similar.

  • Tax advice

    Advice on tax obligations, preparing tax returns and optimising tax costs.

  • Financial reporting

    Preparing annual financial statements and quarterly and monthly reports for management or banks.

  • Preparing and filing tax returns

    Preparing and filing returns with the Tax Administration (VAT, corporate income tax, personal income tax and more).

  • Business and investment advice

    Business analysis, financial risk assessment, planning and budgeting.

  • Loan documentation

    Help with collecting and preparing the documents needed to obtain bank loans.

  • Personal finances and tax for entrepreneurs

    Helping entrepreneurs keep their business books and calculate personal income and taxes.
