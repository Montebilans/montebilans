Consulting

Expert support for management in making business decisions.

Consulting services offered by Montebilans:

  • Tax consulting

    Advice on optimising tax obligations, tax risk analysis, developing tax strategies and support during tax inspections.

  • Financial consulting

    Financial plans and analyses, budgeting, cash flow management and assessing business profitability.

  • Business advisory

    Help with planning and organising the business, optimising business processes and strategies for company growth.

  • Company formation and restructuring

    Advice when setting up new companies and choosing a legal form, and during mergers, acquisitions or restructuring of existing companies.

  • Preparing for financing

    Analysing financial statements, preparing documentation and advice for successfully applying for loans or other financing.

  • Contract preparation and review

    Advice when entering business contracts, assessing financial and legal risks.

  • Transfer pricing

    Advice and transfer pricing studies for related entities in line with legal requirements.

  • Due diligence

    Detailed financial, tax and legal analysis before buying, selling or merging companies.

  • Investment consulting

    Investment feasibility analysis, preparing investment projects and advice on sources of financing.

Accounting agencies that offer consulting give management expert support in making business decisions, reducing risk and improving overall performance.
