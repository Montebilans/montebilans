Bookkeeping

Well-kept books and compliant records, without the worry.

Bookkeeping services offered by Montebilans:

  • Keeping business books

    Recording and posting all business transactions (incoming and outgoing invoices, bank statements, cash, travel orders).

  • VAT calculation

    Preparing and filing VAT returns and keeping VAT records.

  • Payroll and other earnings

    Calculating employee salaries, taxes and contributions, as well as fees under service or copyright contracts.

  • Monthly and annual financial statements

    Balance sheet, income statement, statistical reports and other mandatory reports.

  • Tax returns

    Preparing and filing tax returns (corporate income tax, personal income tax, property tax, annual personal income tax).

  • Subsidiary ledgers

    Supplier ledger, customer ledger, cash book and others.

  • Year-end accounts

    Preparing the annual accounts at the end of the financial year.

  • Archiving business documents

    Keeping documentation orderly and in line with legal regulations.
