Who we are

Montebilans is an accounting agency that has been operating successfully since 2009, providing reliable, high-quality accounting services to small and medium-sized companies. As a family business, we take a personal approach to every client, with dedication, precision and many years of experience.

After rebranding in 2018, we modernised our business and continue to follow the latest standards in accounting. We are proud to work with more than 100 satisfied clients, in Montenegro and abroad, giving them support that makes their business easier and better.
