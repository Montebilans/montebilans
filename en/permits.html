Registration and permits

Work and residence permits for foreign nationals, quickly and by the book.

Registrations and permits offered by Montebilans:

  • Work permits for foreign nationals

    Preparing and submitting work permit applications for foreign workers, including all required documents and communication with the competent authorities (employment office, Ministry of Interior).

  • Work permit renewals

    Managing the renewal or extension of existing work permits before they expire.

  • Residence registration and residence permits

    Helping foreign nationals register temporary residence in Montenegro, including preparing the required documents and submitting applications to the Ministry of Interior.

  • Paying residence taxes

    Calculating and paying residence taxes for foreign nationals and keeping records of taxes paid.

  • Advice on legislation and procedures

    Advice on current laws and regulations on employing foreigners and their residence status.

  • Administrative support for hiring foreigners

    Help with preparing employment contracts, registering foreign workers in the social insurance system and keeping the required documentation.

  • Deregistration and termination of work permits

    Help with terminating employment contracts and deregistering work permits when employment ends.

  • Tracking deadlines and reminders

    Monitoring the validity of work and residence permits and notifying clients in good time about the steps needed to renew them.

Agencies specialised in these services are a great help to companies employing foreign nationals, and to individuals coming to Montenegro to work and live.
